Integration level of all European states

Each state is scored on six pillars: EU membership, single market access, customs union, Schengen, the euro and the area of freedom, security and justice. Full participation counts 1, partial counts 0.5. The score is an indicative index built from the Wikipedia comparison of EU agreements, not an official measure. NATO membership is shown for reference and is not part of the score. Join dates refer to the European Communities or the EU; the Union itself was established on 1 November 1993.
